How it works

  1. Write your name, then choose the module for this week.
  2. Answer the 12 questions. You get the correction straight away.
  3. From 80% correct you receive your certificate.
  4. Take a screenshot of the certificate and upload it to the MS Teams assignment.
Under 80%? No problem — you can start again as often as you like. The Help button costs you no points at all, so use it. Plan about 20 minutes per week.
