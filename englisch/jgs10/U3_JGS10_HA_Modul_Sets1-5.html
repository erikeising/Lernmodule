How it works

  1. Write your name, then open the session for this week.
  2. Answer the 12 questions. You get the correction straight away.
  3. From 80 % you receive your delegate certificate – and a code.
  4. Take a screenshot of the certificate and upload it to the MS Teams assignment.
  5. Write your code in your exercise book. Next week, type your name and your code – the next session opens.
🔒 A session opens only when the session before it is passed with 80 %. Under 80 %? Start again as often as you like. The 💡 Help button costs no points. Plan about 20 minutes per session.
