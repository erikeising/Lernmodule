Help

  • Move: arrow keys or the two arrow buttons. O = overview of all slides. F = full screen. Esc closes this window.
  • Timer: the round clock appears on longer tasks. The coloured part shows the time that is left. Start and pause it with the play button. T starts a 5-minute timer on any slide.
  • Quiz: click an answer or press 1–4. If you are wrong, read the feedback and try again.
  • Writing: your text stays only in this browser tab. Copy it (button) before you close the tab or reload.
